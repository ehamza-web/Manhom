What Is Manjam?

In a world where positions, achievements, and news change every day, accurate information isn't enough — it has to stay current. Manjam is the intelligent engine behind Manhom. Every day, it monitors trusted Arab and international sources to discover what's new about people, companies, educational institutions, awards, and conferences across the region, and brings it all together in one place.

What Does Manjam Do?

Manjam works like a tireless prospector, delivering three core services:

  • Discover the New

    Identifies people and organizations not yet documented and adds them to our knowledge base.

  • Update the Existing

    Tracks changes to existing profiles — a new position, a career move, or a recent award.

  • Connect the Dots

    Links people, organizations, and events to map the region's professional network.

Accuracy You Can Rely On

For us, accuracy matters as much as speed. Manjam cross-checks every piece of information against multiple sources, evaluates the reliability of each source, and intelligently distinguishes between people with similar names. Our specialist editors then review anything that needs verification before it's published. The result: current, trustworthy information you can base your decisions, research, and work on.

  1. Monitor sources
  2. Cross-check & score reliability
  3. Editorial review
  4. Published on Manhom
